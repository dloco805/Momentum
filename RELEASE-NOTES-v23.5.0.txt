Momentum v23.5.0
Released: August 3, 2026

REGIONAL TRADE & TECHNICAL SCHOOLS
- Added eight verified Santa Barbara County and Central Coast career-training providers.
- Added Santa Barbara City College Career Education, Center for Employment Training — Santa Maria,
  Cuesta College Skilled Trades & Technology, Laurus College — Santa Maria, San Luis Obispo Adult
  School culinary training, Santa Barbara County Education Office CTE, Santa Maria Joint Union High
  School District CTE, and Ventura College Career Education.
- Upgraded the existing Lompoc Adult School record to Lompoc Adult School and Career Center with
  healthcare, nursing assistant, skilled-trades, diploma, equivalency, and certification pathways.
- Expanded College Pathways beyond Allan Hancock College with a regional provider browser linked
  directly to each organization in the Community Directory.
- Existing installations receive the new providers automatically without deleting educator-created data.

COMMUNITY MAP REMOVAL
- Removed the built-in Community map tab and its unused map module.
- Replaced embedded organization maps with direct Google Maps links, keeping location lookup useful
  without loading a separate in-app map workspace.

CATEGORY CLEANUP
- Added one shared Agriculture & Environment category and moved related farms, producers,
  agricultural suppliers, vineyards, and 4-H into that category.
- Moved South Side Coffee Co., Lompoc Empty Bowls, American Host Restaurant & Catering,
  and Toro Loco into Food & Drink.
- Small categories now display as one clean organization list instead of creating a separate expandable
  separator for every industry or subcategory.
- Nested separators now appear only when a large category has multiple substantial groups that
  meaningfully improve navigation.
- Existing starter records receive the corrected classifications automatically while custom records remain intact.

PRESERVED FROM v23.4.0
- Verified organization-to-student and opportunity-to-student assignments remain enabled.
- Immediate local-storage saving and assignment validation remain intact.
