Momentum v23.2.0
==================

LOCAL PARTNER EXPANSION

- Added 57 new potential local partners to the automatic Community Directory library.
- Expanded arts and media, construction trades, professional services, healthcare,
  human services, recreation, aerospace, agriculture, and civic organizations.
- New starter records are categorized and color-coded automatically in Community.
- Added richer career fields, services, student-support ideas, addresses, phones,
  and organization-specific websites where a verified public page was available.
- Organizations are marked as potential connections; inclusion does not imply an
  established partnership, internship, volunteer opening, or student availability.

AUTOMATIC UPGRADE

- No Load Partners button is required.
- Existing v23.1.0 users receive only the 57 missing starter records automatically.
- Existing edits, custom partners, archives, and student connections are preserved.
- Reopening the app does not create duplicates.

STARTER DIRECTORY TOTAL

- 164 starter partners after a fresh installation.
