Momentum v21.0.0

Community Directory + Experiences

Community Directory:
- Added 38 more Lompoc-area businesses, nonprofits,
  agencies, education programs, and community organizations.
- Directory now contains well over 90 starter places.
- Grouped first by type/category, then alphabetically.
- Replaced large cards with compact expandable rows.
- Search and filters remain.
- Map is no longer always visible.
- Show on Map opens a large map modal with directions from Maple High School.
- Maple High School is permanently identified with a star and address:
  4010 Jupiter Ave, Lompoc, CA 93436.
- Added direct directions and website buttons.

Student Connections:
- Community Connections now appears on My Momentum.
- Goals remain.
- Added a Next Steps card.
- Connections display the organization and relationship type.
- Missing connections appear red and provide Add Connection.
- Partner assignment student lists are alphabetical.
- Relationship types reduced to:
  Career Interest, Current Internship, Project Connection,
  Visit / L2L, and Potential Partner.

Experiences:
- Opportunities tab renamed Experiences.
- Removed redundant Lompoc business-research cards.
- Businesses remain in Community Directory.
- Added useful local experience ideas organized by type:
  Volunteer, Leadership, Career Program, College, Career Exploration,
  Employment, Entrepreneurship, and Service Project.
- Added local links for LVMC teen volunteering, City parks volunteering,
  FUTURE for Lompoc Youth, Allan Hancock, Maple, and more.
