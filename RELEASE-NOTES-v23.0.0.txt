Momentum v23.0.0

Community Redesign

COMMUNITY DIRECTORY — WHO THEY ARE
- Rebuilt the directory as compact, expandable category and subcategory groups.
- Added a consistent color system across 15 organization categories.
- Added organization summary counts for linked opportunities and student connections.
- Added one live Community search plus category, location, and status filters.
- Expanded organization records with category, subcategory, career fields, and student-support options.
- Upgraded Connect Student with an alphabetical picker showing project, internship,
  community-connection, and open Next Step context.
- Simplified relationship types to Internship, Volunteer, Job Shadow, Mentorship,
  Career Exploration, Employment, College, and Project Partner.
- Reworked the map popup to show Maple High School and the selected organization
  without creating a route or directions.

VOLUNTEER & INTERNSHIPS — WHAT STUDENTS CAN DO THERE
- Reframed Opportunities as specific volunteer, internship, job-shadow, mentorship,
  career-visit, workshop, employment, and career-exploration leads.
- Added color-coded opportunity types, summary metrics, quick filters, compact groups,
  and expandable detail rows.
- Added practical fields for age requirements, schedule, commitment, transportation,
  compensation, capacity, contact information, verification status, and action steps.
- Opportunities can be linked to one organization and opened from either workspace.
- Added 12 Lompoc community inquiry leads. These are clearly marked for verification
  and do not claim that a current placement or opening is guaranteed.
- Preserved student matching and assignment workflows.

COLLEGE PATHWAYS
- Rebuilt Allan Hancock College exploration into nine color-coded pathway groups.
- Added pathway metrics, live search, expandable program lists, and direct program links.
- Added Connect Student so a program can be saved as a postsecondary goal.
- Added an optional College Next Step during program connection.

DATA & COMPATIBILITY
- Existing organization, opportunity, student-connection, and Next Step data remain compatible.
- The combined starter-data action loads both the organization directory and opportunity leads.
- No internet connection is required for Momentum data storage; external maps and program
  websites require internet access when opened.
